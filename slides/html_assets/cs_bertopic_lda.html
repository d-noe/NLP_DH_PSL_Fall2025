LDA (Latent Dirichlet Allocation)
BERTopic
  • Probabilistic generative model of word co-occurrence.
  • Documents are mixtures of topics; topics are word distributions.
  • Assumes “bag-of-words” representation.
  • Leverages transformer embeddings for document semantics.
  • Clusters embeddings (e.g., via HDBSCAN) to find topics.
  • Uses c-TF-IDF to extract representative words per cluster.
  • Term-frequency or TF-IDF matrix (sparse count-based input).
  • No context between words.
  • Sentence/document embeddings (dense contextual vectors).
  • Captures semantics beyond simple co-occurrence.
  • Bayesian inference over topic-word and doc-topic distributions.
  • Typically trained via Gibbs sampling or variational Bayes.
  • Dimensionality reduction (e.g., UMAP) → clustering (HDBSCAN).
  • Topic representation refined with c-TF-IDF and optionally merged.
  • Topic-word probabilities; document-topic probabilities.
  • Interpretable word lists, but may mix semantically close topics.
  • Topic clusters with keywords and representative documents.
  • Can capture fine-grained or semantic topics.
  • Requires predefining n_topics.
  • Works well for large, homogeneous corpora.
  • Efficient, interpretable, but limited by vocabulary and context.
  • Automatically determines number of topics via clustering.
  • Flexible, multilingual, adaptable to embeddings.
  • Less interpretable, depends on embedding quality.
💡 Summary: LDA uncovers word co-occurrence patterns in a count space. BERTopic discovers semantic clusters in an embedding space.
