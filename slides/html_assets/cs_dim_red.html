MDS (Multidimensional Scaling)

  • Preserves pairwise distances.
  • Good for small datasets, metric interpretability.
  • Computationally expensive (to untractable) for large datasets.
Key params: metric, n_init, max_iter

PCA (Principal Component Analysis)

  • Linear projection maximizing variance.
  • Fast, deterministic, interpretable axes.
  • Captures global but not nonlinear structure.

t-SNE (t-Distributed Stochastic Neighbor Embedding)

  • Preserves local neighborhoods.
  • Excellent for cluster visualization.
  • Nonlinear, stochastic, slower.
Key params: perplexity, learning_rate, n_iter

UMAP (Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection)

  • Preserves local + global structure.
  • Scalable and fast.
  • Good for semantic visualization of embeddings.
Key params: n_neighbors, min_dist, metric
💡 Tip: Use PCA for preprocessing and transformation interpretability, t-SNE or UMAP for visualization, MDS for preserving metric geometry.
